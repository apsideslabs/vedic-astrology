What counts as a "planet"

Hindu astrology takes cognizance of nine grahas. The book is explicit that the word is used "in the sense of a celestial body or point which has the property of attraction":

Sanskrit English Note
Ravi the Sun a star
Chandra the Moon a satellite of the earth
Kuja Mars
Budha Mercury
Guru Jupiter
Sukra Venus
Sani Saturn
Rahu Dragon's Head an imaginary point — the concourse of the orbits of the earth and the Moon
Ketu Dragon's Tail the opposite point

Hence the Sun, a star, the Moon, a satellite, and Rahu and Ketu, two imaginary points, are all referred to "under the somewhat forced name of planets". Uranus, Neptune and Pluto "are considered to have no effect on human affairs".

Note on Rahu and Ketu: the book treats them as the nodes of the Moon's orbit, which is exactly what the engine computes. The engine uses the mean node, which is what Raman's own ephemeris tabulates; the true node differs by up to about 1°40′.

Nature and indications

Transcribed from the book, condensed but not altered:

The Sun — masculine, malefic, copper colour, philosophical tendency, royal, ego, father, sons, patrimony, self-reliance, political power, windy and bilious temperament, month, places of worship, money-lenders, goldsmith, bones, fires, coronation chambers, doctoring capacity.

The Moon — mind, mother; benefic when waxing, malefic when waning; feminine, white colour, women, sea-men, pearls, gems, water, fishermen, stubbornness, romances, bath-rooms, blood, popularity, human responsibilities.

Mars — brothers, masculine, blood-red colour, malefic, refined taste, base metals, vegetation, rotten things, orators, ambassadors, military activities, commerce, aerial journeys, weaving, public speakers.

Mercury — benefic if well associated, hermaphrodite, green colour, mercantile activity, public speakers, cold, nervous, intelligence.

Jupiter — masculine, benefic, bright yellow colour, devotion, progeny, truthfulness, religious fervour, philosophical wisdom, corpulence.

Venus — feminine, benefic, mixture of all colours, wife, love affairs, sensual pleasure, family bliss, vitality.

Saturn — malefic, hermaphrodite, dark colour, stubbornness, impetuosity, demoralisation, windy diseases, despondency, gambling.

Rahu — malefic, feminine, renunciation, corruption, epidemics.

Ketu — hermaphrodite, malefic, religious, sectarian principles, pride, selfishness, occultism, mendicancy.

The book adds the governing caution: "Malefics are always inclined to do harm; but under certain conditions, the intensity of the mischief is tempered. Benefics, on the other hand, tend to do good; but sometimes they also become capable of doing harm."

Note that Mercury and the Moon are conditional. Mercury is a benefic only when well associated; the Moon is benefic when waxing and malefic when waning. The engine keeps them in the benefic list (they are benefics by nature) but the reading rules test for affliction separately.

Exaltation

SOURCE: chapter I, "Exaltation". A planet is exalted in a particular sign; within that sign one degree is its point of deep exaltation. The power to do good when exalted is greater than when in its own sign.

Planet Deep exaltation
Sun Aries 10°
Moon Taurus 3°
Mars Capricorn 28°
Mercury Virgo 15°
Jupiter Cancer 5°
Venus Pisces 27°
Saturn Libra 20°
Rahu Taurus 20°
Ketu Scorpio 20°

Results of exaltation as the book gives them:

  • Sun: learned, religious, strong, placid, charitable.
  • Moon: rich, sedulous, a man of letters.
  • Mars: possessing great fervour, educated, famous, princely.
  • Mercury: learned, cheerful, fortunate, respected.
  • Jupiter: chief of men, strong, respected, given to anger, supporting a large number of men.
  • Venus: charitable, lives to a good old age, many qualities.
  • Saturn: skilful, charitable, opulence, long life, loving husband.
  • Rahu or Ketu: wealthy.

Debilitation

SOURCE: chapter I. "The debilitation or depression points are found by adding 180° to the maximum points given above." While in a state of fall, planets give results contrary to those when in exaltation.

So: Sun debilitated in Libra 10°, Moon in Scorpio 3°, Mars in Cancer 28°, Mercury in Pisces 15°, Jupiter in Capricorn 5°, Venus in Virgo 27°, Saturn in Aries 20°, Rahu in Scorpio 20°, Ketu in Taurus 20°.

Moolatrikona

SOURCE: chapter I, "Moolathrikonas". A planet's own special division of its own sign, stronger than mere ownership.

Planet Moolatrikona
Sun Leo 0°–20°
Moon Taurus 4°–20°
Mars Aries 0°–12°
Mercury Virgo 16°–20°
Jupiter Sagittarius 0°–10°
Venus Libra 0°–15°
Saturn Aquarius 0°–20°

STANDARD: most modern references give the Moon's moolatrikona as Taurus 4°–30°. The engine uses the book's 4°–20°; change zodiac.MOOLATRIKONA if you want the other convention. Mars's upper bound is printed as "0° to it" in the scan and is reconstructed as 12°, which is the only value that makes sense of the standard table.

The ten Avasthas (states)

SOURCE: chapter I, "Planetary Avasthas" — "Avasthas are states of existence which planets get when occupying certain positions. They are 10 in number."

Avastha Condition Result given
Deeptha exaltation good progeny, gains, respect from elders, wealth
Swastha own house fame, position, lands, happiness
Muditha a friendly house happiness, good temper, good wife
Santha auspicious divisions strength and courage, comfort and happiness
Sakta retrogression courage, wealth, reputation
Peedya residence in the last quarter of a sign (inauspicious subdivision) incarceration, criminal tendencies, evil nature
Deena unfriendly house mental worry, sickness, degradation
Pikala combustion — "planets very near the Sun become combust" disease, disgrace, loss of children
Khala debilitation losses, mean birth, quarrels, unpleasantness
Bheetha in acceleration losses from various sources, torture, mean habits

Note that retrogression is a strength, not a weakness in this scheme (Sakta: courage, wealth, reputation), and that acceleration is the bad state (Bheetha). That is the opposite of the modern Western instinct, and it matches the book's treatment of Chestabala in chapter III (see 08-strength.md).

The engine implements Deeptha, Swastha, Muditha, Deena, Pikala and Khala directly through its dignity ladder; Santha, Peedya, Sakta and Bheetha are not yet wired into the reading.

Combustion

SOURCE: chapter I mentions the phenomenon only — "planets very near the Sun become combust". STANDARD: the orbs used by the engine are the conventional ones: Moon 12°, Mars 17°, Mercury 14° (12° when retrograde), Jupiter 11°, Venus 10° (8° retrograde), Saturn 15°.

Planetary days and colours

The book uses colour and weekday as signifiers in the dasha and transit chapters (Saturn produces death by chills; Mars by fevers and haemorrhage) rather than as a systematic table, so they are recorded above with each planet's nature and not tabulated separately.
