# 14. What each dasha gives — planet by planet

**SOURCE:** chapter X, "General Results" and the planet-by-planet sections that
follow it.

The book's framing first:

> "A planet during its Dasa generally gives the results pertaining to the houses
> it rules or aspects."

and then, for the *house* a dasha lord occupies:

| Dasha lord in | Result |
|---|---|
| 2nd | success to the family, marriage if not already married, good earnings, generally favourable |
| 3rd | help and co-operation from brothers and relations, honour and approbation |
| 4th | help to relations, acquisition of vehicles, land and houses, higher status |
| 5th | merriment, birth of a son, general prosperity |
| 6th | good health, success over enemies and litigation — or disease, debt and enemies if ill-disposed |
| 7th | marriage if not married, marital happiness, pleasure trips, family happiness |
| 8th | discharge of debts, acquisition of amenities, general good |
| 9th | prosperity, birth of grandchildren, happiness and wealth |
| 10th | professional prosperity, pilgrimage, reputation, respect, realisation of ambitions |
| 11th | influx of wealth, domestic happiness, gains |
| 12th | spending money for good purposes, religious inclination |

"provided the planets are strong and favourable. If they are weak and
unfavourable, the predictions have to be modified."

**Rahu and Ketu** "give effects, good or bad as the case may be, according to the
nature of the planet they associate with or the planet in whose house they may
be situated."

---

## Sun's period — 6 years

**By dignity or sign:**

- **exalted** → wisdom, money, fame, happiness
- **own house, or joined with the lord of the 9th or 10th** → happiness, gains,
  riches, honours
- **with the lord of the 5th** → birth of children
- **related to the lord of the 2nd** → riches, property, gains, favours from
  influential persons
- **debilitated, or in the 6th or 8th, or conjunct evil planets** → evil
  diseases, loss of wealth, reverses in employment, penalty, illness
- **deep exaltation** → sudden gains in cattle and wealth, much travelling in
  eastern countries, residence abroad, quarrels among friends and relations,
  pleasure trips, lovely women
- **moolatrikona** → birth of children, respect from high personages, gains in
  cattle and money, power and political success
- **Leo** → respect from kings and noble personages, righteous conduct, birth of
  children, respect among children
- **Libra** → reduced to poverty, troubled by enemies, failure in undertakings,
  death of property and wealth
- **conjunct the lord of the Lagna** → good gain in the latter half of the dasha,
  pleasant functions, vehicles, travel, respect
- **in the 2nd or 7th** → accidents, illness and other bad results

## Moon's period — 10 years

**By position:**

- **in the Ascendant** → riches and happiness, reputation
- **in a friend's house** → earns well, learns, succeeds
- **in an enemy's house** → unfriendly, vexed by family, loss of money in
  disputes
- **with an exalted planet** → contented and happy
- **with an evil planet** → loss from fire, stealing and judicial orders
- **with a good planet** → happy and successful

**By house:** 2nd → increase in family and riches; 3rd → happiness to self and
brothers, good earning; 4th → death of mother if afflicted, earnings by
cultivation, good name; 5th → children; 6th → unhappiness and litigation;
7th → marriage if unmarried, mental worry; 9th → happy, wealthy, honoured;
10th → easy success and wealth, popularity, trustee of a religious institution;
11th → reputation and friends increase, landed property; 12th → much suffering.

**With the Sun** → quarrels with relatives. **Cancer** → addition to wealth,
success in litigation, unexpected treasure, travels. **Scorpio** → fear from
political heads, destruction of relatives, disappointment, loss of wealth,
liberty and honour at risk, mental uneasiness. **Debilitated** → losses and
troubles from various sources, mental worry. **Moolatrikona** → respect from
sovereigns, many gains, landed property, much happiness.

## Mars' period — 7 years

- **enemy's house** → unhappiness from disputes, displeasure of superiors, bodily
  infirmity
- **friend's house** → acquisition of landed property
- **with a debilitated planet** → good earning and reputation
- **aspected by a good planet** → happy and good effects; **by evil planets** →
  destitution

**By house:** 1st → suffers from thieves and poison; 2nd → favoured by others,
good position, but facial or eye disease; 3rd → happy and respected; 4th → loss
of position, quarrels, worries; 5th → loss of money and children, accident to
children; 6th → quarrels, sorrows, debts, enemies; 7th → unpleasantness with the
partner, urinary or bilious trouble; 8th → suffers from boils, loses position,
travels; 9th → bad to father, danger and losses; 10th → dispirited by failure,
good and bad results in profession depending on strength; 11th → respected,
gains and success; 12th → loss of fortune and children.

## Rahu's period — 18 years

**By house:** 1st → illness, friends and relatives suffer; 2nd → deprived of land
and riches, bad food, financial troubles; 3rd → respected, happy with children,
wealth, wife and brothers; 4th → mother's death or sickness, considerable
suffering; 5th → mental worries; 6th → family suffers from thieves, fire,
superiors and other complications; 7th → endangered by reptiles, partner
suffers; 8th → great danger of losing money; 9th → misfortunes, loss of father or
sickness to him, expenses; 10th → religious interest, interest in the occult;
11th → good in all matters, favour from superiors; 12th → disheartened,
unexpected losses.

> "Rahu will not always produce malefic effects. If he occupies the house of a
> benefic who in turn is well fortified then Rahu will certainly produce good
> results."

The book's worked case: Rahu in the 2nd with Jupiter (lord of the 2nd) in the
10th, aspected by Mars from the 7th. Rahu therefore gives Jupiter's results and
aspects the 4th: "the native began and completed his education (4th house)
during Rahu's period, got married (Mars aspecting from 7th), got a son (because
Jupiter is Putrakaraka), and had beginning of professional career (Jupiter in
the 10th)."

## Jupiter's period — 16 years

- **deep exaltation** → gains, happiness, royal respect, good reputation
- **Cancer** → travels for good, anxious, generally fortunate
- **Capricorn** → disheartened, worry, serving others
- **own house** → birth of sons, success in every undertaking, increase in
  relations, widespread fame, happiness

**By house:** 1st → good health, access to riches; 2nd → increased income and
honours, family happiness; 3rd → good relations with brothers and family, success
in undertakings; 4th → respected, access to lands and houses, general happiness;
5th → official favours, not quite good to children; 6th → (line lost in the
scan); 7th → travels and pilgrimages, marriage if unmarried; 8th → homeless,
physical and mental illness, increase of enemies; 9th → increase of knowledge,
riches, children, generally fortunate; 10th → happy with family, professional
success; 11th → gain of land, family disputes; 12th → suffering and uneasiness.

## Saturn's period — 19 years

> "This Dasa signifies deficiency of thought, dishonour and loss of wealth. It
> tends to expose the native to the displeasure of superiors. These results
> should be predicted when Saturn is unfavourably disposed in the horoscope."

- **Capricorn** → increase in lands, gain in wealth, friendship with illustrious
  personages
- **Aquarius** → travelling in western countries, success in litigation, good
  health
- **enemy's house** → quarrels, loss of position, displeasure of superiors
- **friend's house** → happy in general

**By house:** 1st → physically weak, cerebral complaints, loss of money; 2nd →
suffering, eye trouble, disfavours from superiors, mental disorder if heavily
afflicted; 3rd → loss of brothers or sickness to them, indifferent, gets money;
4th → loss of mother or illness to her, litigation, failure in examinations;
5th → (line lost in the scan); 6th → (line lost in the scan) "suffers losses,
dissolution of partnership if in joint business, wife's health a source of
worry"; 7th → (lost); 8th → generally unfavourable; 9th → loses parents, breaks
in fortune; 10th → results depend on Saturn's strength, misunderstandings and
quarrels with officials; 11th → happiness, gain, generally good; 12th → constant
worries and danger.

## Mercury's period — 17 years

- **exalted** → good earnings, interest in religion and study, helping others,
  lands, good name
- **debilitated** → quarrels, loss in business, reputation at stake
- **with benefics** → much happiness, marriage, birth of children, good profits in
  trade
- **with malefics** → loss of reputation, quarrels with friends and relatives,
  low in others' estimation
- **Gemini** → access to intellectual treasures, successful education, fame and
  wealth, happiness from wife and children, marriage ceremonies
- **Virgo** → favour of royal courts, happy life, undisturbed health, charitable
  activity, unexpected gains

**By house:** 1st → good health, fame, profits, education, reputation; 2nd →
access to wealth, family happiness, redemption from debts; 3rd → auspicious
celebrations at home, jewels, a marriage in the family, good name; 4th → securing
a new house but unsuccessful; 5th → [damaged] earnings, initiation into secret
mantras, happiness, befriending high circles; 6th, 8th or 12th → nervous troubles,
enemies multiply, quarrels, government displeasure, disturbed residence, wavering
mind, danger from fire and thieves, living abroad; 7th → gets a wife, education,
new acquaintances, happiness; 9th → does great things, religiously inclined;
10th → happy, increase of fame; 11th → charity and trade, lands and houses.

> "Mercury will give rise to bad results if placed in the 6th, the 8th or the
> 12th from the Lagna."

## Ketu's period — 7 years

> "Ketu produces favourable results if exalted, otherwise the effects will be
> undesirable."

**By house:** 1st → fever, loss of reputation, mental worry, wounds and cuts;
2nd → much expenditure, sick at heart, eye trouble, family worries, disturbance
from the native place; 3rd → happy but quarrels with brothers; 4th → fear of
thieves and fires, troubles to mother, litigation, generally unhappy; 5th →
spends much, loss of children and father, irreligious; 6th → endangered by
poison, fire and thieves; 7th → suffers mentally and bodily, loses wife and
children, quarrels with relatives; 8th → sickly, partner ill, unnecessary fear;
9th → material benefits absent, religious interest revived; 10th → conveyances,
success in litigation; 11th → happy in business and brothers, bad time for
enemies; 12th → illness, travels, generally bad.

## Venus' period — 20 years

The book opens this section with a correction worth quoting:

> "Venus is supposed by many to give always pleasant results while on the other
> hand all that is evil has been ascribed to Saturn to be realised in his Dasa.
> This view is rather erroneous. Whether Venus, Saturn or any other planet, the
> nature of the results always depends upon the natural and temporal dignities
> or afflictions to which the planets are subject to in the horoscope. No planet
> can ever produce either good, unmixed with evil or only evils unmixed with
> good."

- **Taurus** → a life of ease and indulgence, travel to foreign lands, company of
  beautiful women, material ambition fairly realised, symptoms of venereal
  trouble, lands, houses and conveyances
- **Libra** → royal patronage, reputation, gain of wealth, a period of extreme
  passion
- **exalted** → lost in revelry, gets riches
- **debilitated** → agitated, frustrated, family put to great distress
- **friendly house** → debaucherous life, does good to others, cultivates fine
  arts
- **enemy's house** → sickly, bad for the health of wife and children, quarrels
  with friends and relatives, much expenditure

**By house:** 1st → bodily happiness, increase of reputation; 2nd → spends and
earns well, everyone respects the subject; 3rd → prosperity to brothers and to
the native; 4th → gains, happiness, lands, vehicles, worships mammon and
otherwise happy; 5th → fame, honours, gold, lands, children, ambitions realised;
6th → sickly, troubles from enemies, misunderstandings; 7th → good gains, family
happiness; 8th → venereal troubles, unnecessary travels, eating in others'
houses; 9th → makes superiors happy and benefits from them, generally good;
10th → fortune, honours, education, fame, money, new friends; 11th → happy in
children and wealth, general prosperity; 12th → dispossessed, suffers, goes to
foreign lands, unnecessary quarrels.

## Bhuktis

> "In the sub-period (Bhukti) the lords will have the same influence as in the
> periods stated above. In this case, however, the influences of both planets
> (major lord and sub-lord) are to be compounded as also the relation between
> them taken into account."

## The book's closing caution

> "As already indicated in the beginning of this chapter, the above results are
> of a general character and they should be adapted in such careful manner that
> peculiarities obtaining in horoscopes under consideration are under no
> circumstances omitted or overlooked."

These are **general** results keyed to a single factor — the dasha lord's sign or
house. They are the raw material of a reading, not a reading. The engine presents
them as such: the statement quotes the general result and then reports the dasha
lord's dignity separately, so the reader can see both the rule and its
modification.
