## Peculiarities of the signs

The book lists, for each sign, eight qualities: modality (movable / fixed /
common), parity (odd / even), gender, temperament (cruel / mild), element,
fruitfulness where it states one, ascension (short / long), and the part by
which the sign rises (head / hinder part). Transcribed as given:

| Sign | Modality | Parity | Gender | Temperament | Element | Fruitful | Ascension | Rises by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | movable | odd | masculine | cruel | fiery | — | short | hinder part |
| Taurus | fixed | even | feminine | mild | earthy | fruitful | short | hinder part |
| Gemini | common | odd | masculine | cruel | airy | barren | short | head |
| Cancer | movable | even | feminine | mild | watery | fruitful | long | hinder part |
| Leo | fixed | odd | masculine | cruel | fiery | barren | long | head |
| Virgo | common | even | feminine | mild | earthy | — | long | head |
| Libra | movable | odd | masculine | cruel | airy | — | long | head |
| Scorpio | fixed | even | feminine | mild | watery | — | long | head |
| Sagittarius | common | odd | masculine | cruel | fiery | — | long | hinder part |
| Capricorn | movable | even | feminine | mild | earthy | — | long | hinder part |
| Aquarius | fixed | odd | masculine | cruel | airy | fruitful | short | head |
| Pisces | common | even | feminine | mild | watery | — | short | head and hinder part |

Where the book is silent on fruitfulness (Aries, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius,
Capricorn, Pisces) the table leaves it blank rather than filling it in from
elsewhere.

Note that the book's ascension list differs from the textbook "signs of short
ascension" (Capricorn–Gemini) because it is describing the *rising part* in
the Indian context; it is reproduced, not corrected.

## Signs and the body

**SOURCE:** chapter V — "the twelve signs of the zodiac commencing from Aries
in general and from the Ascendant in particular govern the twelve important
organs of the human body".

| Sign | Organ |
|---|---|
| Aries | head |
| Taurus | face |
| Gemini | neck |
| Cancer | heart |
| Leo | stomach |
| Virgo | waist |
| Libra | lower belly |
| Scorpio | sexual organs |
| Sagittarius | thighs |
| Capricorn | knees |
| Aquarius | buttocks |
| Pisces | feet |

The book applies this in both directions: read from Aries for the body in
general, and read from the Ascendant for the native in particular.

**STANDARD:** the common Kalapurusha list assigns Gemini to the arms and
shoulders, Cancer to the chest, and Aquarius to the calves. The engine uses
the book's list, and `zodiac.SIGN_BODY_PART` is the single place to change it.

## How the sign qualities are used

The book uses each of these qualities as a filter on a rule, never on its own:

- **Modality** decides temperament and timing. Movable signs indicate
  executive ability, a pioneering spirit, ambition and capacity to make
  headway against difficulties. Fixed signs indicate stability, patience,
  endurance and diplomacy, and "persons born in these signs will have a
  fixity of purpose and determination — they make pages of history." Common
  signs indicate versatility, flexibility and lack of originality: superficial
  knowledge of many things, without persistence. (ch. V)
- **Modality in horary** fixes speed: movable signs mean speedy realisation,
  common signs early consummation, fixed signs uncertainty "extending to
  years". (ch. XI)
- **Element** decides the temperament when a majority of planets fall in it:
  fiery → inspirational and ardent; airy → intellectual; watery → emotional;
  earthy → practical. (ch. V)
- **Element of the 10th** decides the profession. (ch. VII, see
  [10-interpretation.md](10-interpretation.md))
- **Gender of a sign or planet** decides the sex of children and the sex of
  the partner described. A masculine planet ruling the house of children
  gives male issues during its period. (ch. I, IX)
- **Movable signs and Saturn** together decide adoption and fosterage. (ch.
  VIII)
