Why this one

The astrology software most people meet is a black box: it prints a chart and a verdict, and you cannot tell where either came from. This is the opposite. The rules are written out in the knowledge base, the code implements exactly those rules, and the reading tags each statement with the chapter it came from — so you can look up R-MARRIAGE-MARS and see the sentence in chapter IX that produced it.

It is also checked against something objective. The book works a chart out by hand in chapter II, and this engine reproduces it:

What it does not do

It implements one 1930s primer and nothing more: no Ashtakavarga, no full Shadbala, no divisional charts beyond D9, no compatibility matching, no muhurta. Where the scanned book is damaged, the transit tables show a gap rather than a guess. Two of the book's rules — the navamsa count for the number of children, and the Sade Sati third-passage claim — are documented but deliberately not implemented.
