The book works a chart out by hand in chapter II — 2 November 1935, 05:20 local mean time at Madras. The engine reproduces it from first principles:

The residual is the book's own ayanamsa, which it rounds to 21°30′; the engine computes 21°30′55″ for that date. The Rasi chart, the dignities, the Mars–Jupiter exchange and the Vimshottari balance of 6y 1m 15d match too, and all of it is asserted in the test suite.

BWhat it will not do

It implements one 1930s primer and nothing else: no Ashtakavarga, no full Shadbala, no divisional charts beyond D9, no compatibility matching, no muhurta. Two of the book's rules — the navamsa count for the number of children, and the Sade Sati third-passage claim — are documented but deliberately not implemented, because both would be read as a prediction about a real family or a real death.
