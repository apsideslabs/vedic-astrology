13. Horary (Prasna)

SOURCE: chapter XI, "Horary or Answering Questions".

What it is

"Prasna or Horary Astrology consists of divination by means of planets. This branch of Astrology will be really useful as it enables us to answer questions arising in everyday life in movements of the profoundest anxiety — when life or fortune may be trembling in the balance."

The method: cast a chart for the moment the question is asked, place the planets in Rasi and Navamsa, estimate their strength, and read the houses that govern the matter asked about. There is no birth data at all — the question time is the chart.

The book says it learned the rules from Chappanna and Jinendramala and found them "unfailing", and that "Horary Astrology can be quickly learned by any person of moderate ability and may be well understood and reduced to constant practice."

The engine supports this directly: feed the question time as the birth data and ignore the natal interpretation, reading only the chart.

The typical questions, with the book's rules

(1) Whether an event to occur suddenly will be good or bad? If the rising sign is Leo, Virgo, Libra, Aquarius, Scorpio or Gemini and the rising lord is beneficial, the event will be fortunate. Otherwise, unfavourable.

(2) Will my health improve? If the Ascendant lord is in the 5th or 9th, joined or aspected by benefics, and the Ascendant is free from malefic aspects — yes. If the Ascendant lord is in the 6th, 8th or 12th and afflicted — no.

(3) Whether the querent shall become rich? The lord of the 2nd conjunct or in good aspect with the lord of the 9th, with the 2nd house free from the aspect of the 6th, 8th or 12th lord. The nature of the rising sign and Navamsa — movable, common or fixed — reveals the time: movable signs denote speedy realisation, common signs early consummation, fixed signs uncertainty extending to years.

(4) Is it advisable to undertake a certain journey? If the Ascendant lord or the Moon is strong and in the 3rd, or in reception with the lord of the 3rd, the journey may safely be undertaken. Otherwise not.

(5) Will it be advisable to sell or buy a certain property? If the Ascendant lord, the 7th lord and the 4th lord are well situated, the transaction may be done with advantage. Jupiter or Venus in the 4th → a profitable investment. Saturn there → no good to buyer or seller.

(6) Is it advisable to move from one place to another? If the 7th house and its lord are well disposed and free from affliction, the move is favourable. Otherwise not.

(7) Will the patient recover at an early date? Lords of the 1st and 6th conjunct → unlikely to recover. A common sign in the 6th → average duration; a movable sign → short sickness; a fixed sign → long and hard to cure.

(8) Is there any treasure in the ground? If the 2nd is Taurus, Libra or Cancer with the Moon and Venus, or Mars and Rahu, the chances are remote. If the 4th is Cancer with Jupiter and the Moon in it, the ground contains a treasure trove. If Mars or Rahu is associated with the 4th, the questioner will not get access to it though it may be present.

(9) Whether the child to be born will be male or female? If the Ascendant is a masculine sign and the 5th is a masculine sign, a boy; otherwise a girl. If the 5th lord is associated with or aspected by a masculine planet, a boy.

(10) Shall I win a prize? Benefics in 3, 5, 7 and 11, with the lords of the 2nd, 9th and 11th strong and free of the 6th and 8th lords.

(11) Whether the questioner shall marry a certain person, and when? Saturn in the 7th → marriage within three months of the question. Moon in 3, 5, 7 or 11 aspected by Jupiter, the Sun and Mercury, with benefics in quadrants or trines → very early. The Ascendant and 7th lords must be well disposed to each other.

(12) Will the stolen article be recovered? A powerful benefic in the 11th from the Prasna Lagna, or aspected by Jupiter or Venus → speedy recovery. Ascendant in a fixed sign or fixed Navamsa → the article was stolen by a near relative. The direction is given by the most powerful planet in the quadrants at the time of the query, or failing planets, by the rising sign.

The book's worked case

On 24 August 1938 at 5h 57m p.m. at Bangalore, a friend who had lost an article put the question. From the Prasna chart the author concluded that "if there was any truth in Horary Astrology, the querent had lost some money, that the person involved was his own employee and that with effort the money would be recovered". The forecast was verified: "upon the clue furnished by the author, the person who stole the money was discovered and the money, recovered."

That is the book's own evidence for horary, offered as an anecdote. It is recorded as the book reports it and nothing more.

The book's warning

"The application of Horary Astrology to idle questions should be deprecated and it should be applied to affairs of great importance and at times of deep anxiety. Astrology sank to a low ebb owing to the absurd use made of this branch and it is our earnest desire that Horary Astrology should never become 'popular' in that sense."

Any app that adds a "ask the stars anything" box is doing the thing the book asks it not to do. If you build one, put this paragraph next to it.
