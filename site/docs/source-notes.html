The source

B. V. Raman, Astrology for Beginners — 135 pages, 12 chapters and two appendices. Published by UBS Publishers' Distributors Ltd., 5 Ansari Road, New Delhi 110 002.

The file this knowledge base was extracted from is a scan of the book held by the Internet Archive:

title:    Astrology for Beginners - BV Raman
creator:  Digitized by the Internet Archive
producer: Recoded by LuraDocument PDF v2.65
created:  2017-05-07
pages:    135
size:     2.29 MB

The scan carries an OCR text layer of about 147,000 characters. Every page has text; pages 1, 6, 8 and 10 are near-empty (the cover plate and blank versos), and pages 60–63 are duplicated in the source scan (page 60 repeats as 62, page 61 as 63).

What was done to it

  1. The text layer was extracted page by page.
  2. The running watermark (jyotishbooks.tk, jyoti hb ok;) was stripped.
  3. The one-word-per-line OCR layout was reflowed into paragraphs.
  4. The chapters were segmented by page range and read in full.
  5. The content was reorganised by subject rather than by chapter, because the book returns to the same topics repeatedly (friendship appears in I and III, the Navamsa in II and IX, Saturn in IV, X and XII).
  6. Every table was transcribed by hand from the reflowed text.

Nothing was paraphrased into a claim the book does not make. Where the book is wrong or dated, it is marked CAUTION rather than silently corrected.

What is in the book but not in this repository

  • Ashtakavarga — chapter XII refers to Raman's separate book on it. Not implemented.
  • Full Shadbala — chapter III refers to Graha and Bhava Balas. Not implemented; see 08-strength.md for what is implemented instead.
  • Varshaphala, muhurta, electional astrology — other books in the series.
  • The Navamsa-count methods for the number of brothers and the number of children — the rules are recorded in 10-interpretation.md but deliberately not implemented, because they are the rules most likely to be read as a prediction about a real family.
  • The Sade Sati first/second/third passage reading — recorded in 12-transits.md, not implemented; see the caution there.

What is not in the book but is in this repository

Flagged STANDARD wherever it appears:

  • the natural friendship rows for Rahu and Ketu;
  • the combustion orbs;
  • the fuller house significations;
  • the conventional Parashari exaltation degrees for Rahu and Ketu (Taurus 20° and Scorpio 20° are actually the book's, but they are the standard values too);
  • the IAU 2006 precession polynomial behind the Lahiri ayanamsa;
  • the mean-node formula (Meeus, ch. 47);
  • the ascendant and midheaven formulae;
  • the Porphyry/Sripati house cusps;
  • the panchanga (the book does not treat it at all).

Verifying the extraction

The strongest check available is that the book carries a fully worked example, and the engine reproduces it. Chapter II prints the nine grahas and the Ascendant for a birth at Madras on 2 November 1935; tests/test_engine.py asserts every one of them, plus the Rasi chart's structure, the dignities, the Mars–Jupiter exchange, the navamsa rule, and the Vimshottari balance of 6y 1m 15d.

If a transcription error had crept into the exaltation table, the ayanamsa, the nakshatra spans, the navamsa formula or the dasha arithmetic, those tests would fail. They pass to within a couple of arcminutes.
