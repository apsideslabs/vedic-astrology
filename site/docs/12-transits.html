Document 12 · ch. XII

SOURCE: chapter XII, "Transits or Gochara".

The principle

"In predicting Gochara results the Moon and the birth constellation play a prominent part. The sign in which the Moon is situated in the birth horoscope is the Janma Rasi. The passage of planets through certain angular positions from the radical Moon is technically termed Gochara."

So every gochara result is read from the Janma Rasi, not from the Ascendant. A planet transiting the sign the Moon occupied at birth is "in the 1st house" for gochara purposes, whatever it is doing in the Rasi chart.

The book's physical account of why transits matter: planetary movement "gives rise to the stimulation of radioactive disintegration which on falling on the individual produces psycho-physical changes in his character, disposition and surroundings". That is the book's model, not a finding.

Sade Sati

"Regarding Saturn, his transit in the 12th, the 1st and the 2nd from the radical Moon has special significance as it goes under the technical name of Sade Sati (7½ years' Saturn), as, to pass through the signs, in his usual course it takes 7½ years. This particular period is dreaded by many as highly malefic; unless the ruling period is powerful, the subject of the horoscope is bound to suffer unexpected miseries."

The summarised results for those three houses: "quarrels and misunderstandings, implication, entanglement in litigation, failure of business, restlessness of mind, change of place, sickness among family members, death of children and mental worry."

And the book's striking claim about repetition:

"One having good longevity can come under the sway of '7½ years' Saturn' thrice in his lifetime. During the first time most of the above results will manifest; the second time, no evil results will occur while during the third time the death of the native is invariably caused, unless ruling Dasa does not warrant death."

CAUTION: the third-passage claim is the single most alarming sentence in the book. It is not something any software should assert about a person. The engine reports Sade Sati as active or not, with the source's description of the period, and stops there — it does not implement the first/second/third passage reading at all.

How to weight transits

"Of the different planets, the transiting effects of the slow moving ones, viz., Jupiter, Saturn and Rahu are universally held to be most powerful. The triennial influence may be read from Saturn, the yearly influence from Jupiter, the monthly influence from the Sun and Mercury, and the daily influence from the Moon."

The engine's reading reports Jupiter, Saturn and Rahu (and Ketu, which moves with Rahu) by default, for exactly this reason.

Degree conditions and exceptions

"There are exceptions under which certain planets said to produce malefic effect while transiting certain houses, do not manifest anything unfavourable at all."

"Mars and the Sun produce effects during their transit when they are in the first 10 degrees of a sign. Jupiter and Venus become effective when they are in the 2nd ten degrees while Rahu produces results throughout the entire sign."

So a transit result only bites in a particular third of the sign for some planets. The engine reports the degree in sign so that this can be applied by the reader; it does not gate on it.

"Timing events according to transits in the light of the Ashtakavarga has been enumerated in my book The Ashtakavarga System of Prediction."

Ashtakavarga is not in this book and is not implemented.

The standing rule

"The Gochara results should never be interpreted without reference to the ruling period and sub-period."

Every transit reading this engine produces carries that sentence with it.
