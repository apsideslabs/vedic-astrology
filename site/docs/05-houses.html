Document 05 · ch. I

SOURCE: chapter I, "The Twelve Houses" and "Kinds of Houses".

Houses are not signs

This is the first thing the book insists on:

"The twelve houses are not necessarily coincident with the twelve signs of the zodiac. They are, in fact, variable. Each sign is always 30° in extent. But the length of a house or Bhava depends upon the time of birth and the latitude of the place of the birth."

Two different things are therefore being drawn at once, and the book uses both:

  1. The Rasi chakra — whole signs. The rising sign is the first bhava and each following sign is the next bhava. Chapter II says that "for ordinary purposes of prediction a zodiacal diagram (Rasi Chakra) with the Ascendant marked is sufficient".
  2. The bhava chakra — houses of unequal extent, whose mid-points (bhavamadhyas) are obtained by taking Western house cusps and deducting the ayanamsa.

The engine defaults to whole signs, and offers Sripati (Porphyry) cusps and equal houses for the second reading. See 07-calculation.md.

The significations

SOURCE: chapter I, transcribed as printed (the scan loses a few words, and the gaps are shown rather than guessed):

House Significations
1st birth, body, appearance
2nd family, source of death [sic], property, vision
3rd intelligence, brothers, sisters
4th vehicles, general happiness, education, mother and landed properties
5th fame, children
6th debts, diseases, misery, enemies
7th wife or husband, tact, death
8th longevity, gifts
9th God, guru, father, travel, piety
10th occupation, Karma, philosophical knowledge
11th gains
12th losses, moksha

CAUTION: "source of death" in the 2nd and "death" in the 7th are the book's own shorthand — it treats the 2nd and 7th as maraka (death- inflicting) houses in chapter IV. It is not a claim that the 2nd house causes death.

STANDARD: the fuller lists a practitioner expects are these, and the engine's zodiac.HOUSE_SIGNIFICATIONS uses a merged version:

  • 2nd: family, speech, wealth, food, right eye
  • 3rd: siblings, courage, communication, short journeys, hands
  • 4th: mother, home, land, vehicles, education, happiness
  • 5th: children, intelligence, past merit, romance, authority
  • 6th: enemies, disease, debt, service, litigation, maternal uncle
  • 7th: spouse, partnership, business, public dealings
  • 8th: longevity, sudden events, inheritance, occult, chronic illness
  • 9th: father, guru, dharma, fortune, long journeys, higher learning
  • 10th: career, status, karma, authority, father (in some schools)
  • 11th: gains, elder siblings, friends, fulfilment of desires
  • 12th: loss, expenditure, foreign lands, moksha, sleep, confinement

Kinds of houses

SOURCE: chapter I, "Kinds of Houses".

Group Houses Sanskrit
Quadrants / angles 1, 4, 7, 10 Kendras
Trines 1, 5, 9 Trikonas
Succeedent 2, 8, 11 Panaparas
Cadent 3, 6, 12 Apoklimas

And the strength rule attached to them:

"Planets in angles generally become fairly strong; in succeedent houses slightly strong and in cadent houses, utterly weak. In trines, they become very strong."

That is the whole basis of the engine's house-placement term in strength.positional_strength, and it is why trines carry a bonus on top of the kendra term.

CAUTION: the 6th, 8th and 12th are also the dusthanas, the houses of difficulty, which the book does not name as a group but treats as such throughout ("lords of 3, 6, 8 and 11 do evil and cause miseries").

Karakas of the houses

SOURCE: chapter I. Each planet is the Karaka of some matter:

Matter Karaka
father Sun
mother Moon
brother Mars
profession Mercury
children Jupiter
wife or husband Venus
longevity Saturn
maternal relations Rahu
paternal relations Ketu

The book pairs each karaka with the house that governs the same matter, and a judgement requires all three to be examined together: the house, its lord, and the karaka. Chapter IX says it plainly for children — "Favourable disposition of all these three factors is a great asset for getting good, long-lived and obedient children" — and chapter VIII does the same for the parents (4th house, its lord and the Moon; 9th house, its lord and the Sun).

Reading a house

SOURCE: chapter III, "Reading a House". A house is judged from:

  1. the strength, aspect and conjunction of the lord of the house;
  2. the planets occupying it;
  3. the planets aspecting it;
  4. the sign on it and the lord of that sign's condition;
  5. the same factors in the Navamsa;
  6. the same factors counted from the Moon as well as from the Ascendant.

The book repeats "from the Ascendant as well as from the Moon" in chapters IV, VI, VII, VIII, IX and XII. Any implementation that reads only from the Lagna is doing half the job; the engine exposes moon_sign() and the Navamsa sign of every planet so that a caller can do both.

Houses counted from other houses

The book uses derived houses freely and calls them out explicitly:

  • the father's finances are "the 2nd from the 9th", his ills "the 6th from the 9th", his profession "the 10th from the 9th" (chapter VIII);
  • children are judged from the 5th from the Ascendant or from the Moon (chapter IX);
  • the Moon's house and "the 8th from the Moon's sign" decide longevity (chapter IV);
  • the whole of gochara is read from the Janma Rasi, i.e. from the Moon (chapter XII).
