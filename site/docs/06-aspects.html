Document 06 · ch. I

SOURCE: chapter I, "Drishti or Aspect".

The basic rule

"For a beginner it is enough to know that according to Hindu Astrology, aspects are counted from sign to sign. An aspect is good or bad according to the relation between the aspecting and the aspected body."

Sign to sign, not degree to degree. A planet in Aries aspects the whole of the 7th sign from it (Libra) and everything in it. That is why the engine's aspects list is built from sign distances and carries no orb.

The grades of sight

The book gives a graded sight, which is the drishti pinda used in the stronger calculations:

Distance Sight
3rd, 10th a quarter sight (0.25)
5th, 9th a half sight (0.5)
4th, 8th three-quarters of a sight (0.75)
7th a full sight (1.0)

Special aspects

"Saturn, Jupiter and Mars have special aspects. Saturn powerfully aspects the 3rd and the 10th houses, Jupiter the 5th and the 9th houses, and Mars, the 4th and the 8th."

Every graha has the full 7th aspect. In addition:

Planet Additional full aspects
Mars 4th, 8th
Jupiter 5th, 9th
Saturn 3rd, 10th
Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Rahu, Ketu none — 7th only

The engine implements this in zodiac.SPECIAL_ASPECTS; the partial-sight fractions are in zodiac.PARTIAL_SIGHT and are reported per house by Chart.aspected_houses.

The book's worked illustration

"If the Sun is in Cancer and Saturn in Taurus, we say that the Sun is in the 3rd from Saturn and consequently receives the powerful aspect (3rd house aspect) of Saturn."

Worth checking the arithmetic: Taurus → Cancer is 3 signs, so Cancer is the 3rd from Taurus, and Saturn's special 3rd-house aspect falls there. The engine reproduces this — see test_aspects_include_special_ones.

How to judge an aspect

The book gives these rules for reading an aspect once found:

  1. By nature of the aspecting planet. "The opposition (7th house) aspect becomes extremely good when it is produced by Jupiter and the Moon. It is held to be good when benefics aspect each other." "As a natural malefic his [Saturn's] aspects are bad. But they produce good in case Saturn acquires beneficence by temporal situations."
  2. By ownership. "If Mars is the lord of the 4th and the 9th houses, he gets special qualifications to produce good aspects (by the 4th house aspect)." A planet aspecting its own house — by the 7th or by a special aspect — "will naturally increase the signification of that house". The book's example: "Venus aspecting the 7th house gives a good and fair-looking wife and happiness on that account."
  3. By relation. See 04-relationships.md: the aspect is good or bad according to the relation between the aspecting and the aspected body.
  4. By the nature of the aspect. The 4th-house aspect of Mars is "a square aspect and sometimes this is evil and sometimes beneficial"; Jupiter's 5th and 9th are trines and therefore benefic in nature.
  5. The opposition has the same power as conjunction. "The opposition aspect has the same power to do good or bad as conjunction, according to the nature of the planet involved."

Where aspects are used downstream

  • Strength. Drukbala: "Aspects of benefics give full Drukbala while aspects of malefics take away Drukbala" (chapter III).
  • Papakarthari and Subhakarthari — the Ascendant hemmed in by malefics or benefics (chapter III).
  • Marriage: "If the 7th house is hemmed in between two malefics (Papakarthari Yoga), then married life proves burdensome and miserable" (chapter IX).
  • Health: "If the Moon is in Cancer or Scorpio in Rasi or in Navamsa and is powerfully aspected by malefics, the person suffers from diseases in the anus" (chapter V).
  • Longevity: "The Ascendant and the 8th from the Moon's sign being aspected by malefics" produces Balarishta (chapter IV).
  • The dasha: "a planet during its Dasa generally gives the results pertaining to the houses it rules or aspects" (chapter X).
