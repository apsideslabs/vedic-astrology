Document 10 · ch. IV–IX

SOURCE: chapters IV to IX. This is the working core of the book.

The book's own frame, stated at the start of chapter III:

"Predictions can be confidently ventured provided one is well equipped with principles that determine strengths of planets and houses."

And its caution, at the end of chapter V:

"No cut-and-dry rules can be laid out for evaluation of these factors as sometimes there will be contradictory forces operating for whose proper reconciliation and interpretation experience is the safest guide."

10.1 Longevity (chapter IV)

The four divisions of life:

Division Span
Balarishta (infant mortality) death before 8 years
Alpayu (short life) death between 8 and 32
Madhyayu (middle life) death between 33 and 75
Pumayu (full life) 75 to 120

The general rule: if the Ascendant and the Ascendant lord are powerful, good longevity may be pronounced. If the Lagna, the Sun and the Moon are all afflicted, the span will be very poor.

The five Balarishta combinations, as listed:

  1. The Moon in the 8th, Mars in the 7th and Rahu in the 9th.
  2. The Moon in an angle with malefics.
  3. The Moon in the Ascendant, Mars in the 8th, the Sun in the 9th and Saturn in the 12th.
  4. The Sun in the Ascendant with Saturn and Rahu, and Mars in the 8th.
  5. The Ascendant and the 8th from the Moon's sign being aspected by malefics.

The antidote: "The strong positions of Jupiter, Mercury and Venus act as antidotes for Balarishta."

The kendra/panapara/apoklima test: according as the benefics and the lord of the Lagna are all posited in kendras, panaparas or apoklimas, life is long (Pumayu), medium (Madhyayu) or short (Alpayu). "If the lord of the 8th house and malefics occupy similar positions, the reverse should be predicted."

The friendship test: examine the friendship between (1) the lord of the house occupied by the Moon and that of the 8th from the Moon; (2) the lord of the Lagna and that of the 8th from the Moon; (3) the lord of the house occupied by the Sun and the lord of the 8th. Friendly → long-lived; inimical → short-lived; neutral → medium.

The Ayushkaraka: "The 3rd and 8th are houses of longevity. Saturn is the indicator of life (Ayushkaraka). The strength of the 8th house, the 3rd house, their lords and Saturn and the Ascendant will add to long life. The conjunction of the 8th lord with Saturn is also good for long life."

Death and its manner: death occurs in the period of (a) planets in the 2nd and 7th; (b) the lords of the 2nd and 7th; (c) planets conjunct those lords; (d) failing all that, the most malicious planet, or the lords of the 3rd and 8th. "Saturn, when he gets the power to kill, inflicts death in preference to any other planet."

The manner of death, from the 8th house and its occupants:

Factor Manner
benefics in or aspecting the 8th a peaceful, normal end amid congenial surroundings
afflicted 8th (22nd Drekkana) the contrary
Rahu in the 8th danger of long suffering, and simulated death
Saturn chills, colds, long suffering, heart failure
Mars fevers, inflammatory action, haemorrhage
planets in fixed signs in the 8th heart, throat or excretory troubles
planets in cardinal signs disease of head, stomach, kidneys or skin
planets in common signs lungs, bowels, nervous system
Rahu assassination
Mars cuts, accidents, burns, effusion of blood
Saturn in watery signs drowning

"The nature of planets and the nature of signs should be blended together."

CAUTION — the most important caution in this repository. These are 1930s longevity rules stated for beginners, and the book itself calls the subject "vexed" and defers full treatment to another volume. The engine reports a longevity indicator and attaches an explicit caveat statement (R-LONGEVITY-CAVEAT) that no software should strip out. Nothing here predicts anyone's death, and no reading should be presented to a person as if it did.

10.2 Appearance, character, mind (chapter V)

The rule: "The sign rising at the time of birth and the planets situated or aspecting the Ascendant and the strength or weakness of the Ascendant lord determine the personal appearance and character of the child."

The twelve rising-sign descriptions are transcribed in interpret.LAGNA_APPEARANCE and in the table in the engine's output. They are the book's own wording, condensed.

Modifications: "The native will correspond in his appearance to the lord of the Navamsa rising or his appearance will be similar to the strongest planet in the horoscope. Generally speaking, Jupiter, Venus, the Sun and Mars, either as lords or close to or aspecting Lagna, impart to the person their respective characteristics."

Planet on or aspecting the Lagna Effect
Sun nobleness of figure, increased health
Moon better proportion, greater delicacy
Mars fair ruddiness, healthy constitution, sturdy figure, hot and dry temperament
Mercury proportionate, well shaped stature, hot temperament, yellowish complexion
Jupiter (the scan loses the line; the standard reading is an expansive, dignified figure)
Venus softness and greater delicacy, "more applicable to female beauty"
Saturn black and curly hair, tall figure, inactive and narrow chest

Mind: "In regard to the mind the Moon's position is important. The Moon in conjunction with malefics and otherwise afflicted will make the subject inclined to insanity, pessimistic and always worried, wicked, morally degraded also. To recapitulate, the affliction of the Moon and Mercury is co-existent with a quick, restless and imaginative temperament most liable to mental disturbance. It holds equally [true] of the affliction of the Sun and Ascendant as regards the physical constitution."

Character by modality and element — see 02-signs.md.

10.3 Health (chapter V)

Rule Indication
Ascendant lord connected with the 6th house or its lord a sickly constitution
malefics in the 6th always indicates illness
Sun in the 6th aspected by malefics enteric and bowel disorders
Moon in Cancer or Scorpio (Rasi or Navamsa) powerfully aspected by malefics diseases of the anus
Moon hemmed in between Saturn and Mars, with the Sun in Capricorn asthma
Ketu, Saturn and Mars in the 6th, 7th and 8th from the Lagna consumption
Moon in the 6th, Ascendant hemmed in between Rahu and Saturn consumption
weak Moon in the 12th with Saturn insanity

"The Ascendant must be powerful and should not be connected with the 6th house or its lord in any way to give sound health."

The book illustrates with a chart where the lord of the Ascendant is in the 7th hemmed between Ketu and Mars, the 6th lord is in the 12th with Rahu, and Ketu, Saturn and Mars occupy the 6th, 7th and 8th: "The native died in her 25th year after suffering from consumption for nearly one year."

CAUTION: these are astrological correspondences, not diagnoses. The engine repeats none of them as fact about a person and the reading carries a disclaimer.

10.4 Education (chapter VI)

"Education is generally ascertained from the 4th house, its lord and the Vidyakaraka or lord of education, viz., Jupiter."

Combination Result
malefics in the 4th broken education
Jupiter and Mercury well situated good knowledge of men and matters
Jupiter in the 4th or 10th high legal education
lord of the 2nd well disposed a good speaker and lecturer
Mercury in an angle, or Venus in the 2nd good knowledge of astrology
Mars in the 2nd with Mercury in an angle a good mathematician, or skill in engineering
Sun or Mars as lord of the 2nd, conjunct Venus or Jupiter proficiency in logic and mental sciences
Jupiter and Venus in angles aspected by Mercury insight into philosophical subjects
Jupiter and Venus in angles a versatile genius
lord of the 4th and Jupiter free of the lords of 3, 6 and 11 a sound educational career

10.5 Wealth (chapter VI)

"Look to the 5th, the 2nd, the 9th and the 11th houses for financial prospects."

Wealth-giving:

  • a benefic, particularly Jupiter, in the 2nd, with the lord of the 2nd in a trine or quadrant, or conjunct benefic lords, or in the 11th → "considerable wealth";
  • the lord of the 2nd in an angle from the Ascendant lord, or the 2nd lord benefic and exalted → good fortune and success;
  • Jupiter as lord of the 2nd, occupying the 2nd, or conjunct Mars → "considerable fortune";
  • Mercury in the 5th with the Moon and Mars in the 11th → "immense wealth";
  • lord of the Ascendant in the 2nd, lord of the 2nd in the 11th, lord of the 11th in the Ascendant → "immense wealth which will be self-earned".

Poverty or difficulty:

  • any planet in the 2nd and 11th badly aspected, with the 2nd lord conjunct the lords of 3, 6 and 11, or otherwise afflicted → difficulty in getting money, "stress and even poverty";
  • Saturn in the 2nd → "reversal of fortune. Such persons, no doubt, earn a lot of money but they will lose everything";
  • Rahu, Ketu or Saturn in the 2nd → "complications and an involved state of finance, and frequently it shows loss by fraud and imposition, many ups and downs of fortune and unexpected rises and falls".

Source of income: "The source of earning should be gleaned from the situation of the 2nd lord. If, for instance, the 2nd lord is in the 9th, inheritance is shown. Legacies are shown by good planets, gain by marriages is shown by benefic planets, particularly Venus in the 7th house."

10.6 Livelihood (chapter VII)

"Generally the profession that one pursues is derived from a consideration of the 10th house (either from the Ascendant or from the Moon whichever is powerful) and its lord, planets in the 10th house, the dominant planet in the horoscope and the situation of the 10th lord in Navamsa."

By the element of the 10th:

Element Profession
fiery factories, fires, army, iron, metallurgy, printing
watery sailors, navigators, admirals, inn-keepers, wine and fish mongers
earthy landed properties, agriculture, cloth shop, trade, gardening
airy orators, journalists, astrologers, technical avocations

By the planet: (the book lists the Sun's, Moon's, Mars's, Mercury's, Jupiter's, Venus's and Saturn's significations — see interpret.TENTH_LORD_CAREER, transcribed from chapter VII.)

By the lord of the Navamsa occupied by the 10th lord — the book's most specific technique:

Navamsa lord Calling
Sun trader in drugs, chemicals and gold
Moon agriculturist, pearls, serving the feminine sex
Mars officer, mechanic, dealer in dangerous weapons
Mercury writer, author, mathematician, sculptor
Jupiter preceptor, priest, lawyer, solicitor
Venus dealer in cattle, dressmaker, dancer, artist
Saturn a menial and subordinate officer

"If two or three planets join, appropriate blending of the influences must be made."

Timing: "Starting of one's professional career, periods of prosperity, etc., should be determined during the periods and sub-periods of planets which may have anything to do with the 10th house, its lord and planets posited in the 10th house."

10.7 Parents, brothers, enemies, debts (chapter VIII)

Parents: the indicators are the Moon (mother) and the Sun (father); the 4th house rules the mother and the 9th the father. Judge each house, its lord and its karaka together.

Rule Indication
malefics in the 4th or 9th affect the parent concerned
the Sun afflicted trouble to the father, possibly a cold severe father
Sun in the 9th with Saturn early death of the father
Saturn in the 4th with the Moon early death of the mother
affliction to the luminaries from Mars accidents to the parents

Derived houses: "The parent's affairs may, to a large extent, be judged by taking the appropriate house as the first, and then proceeding thus. The financial affairs of the father, for instance, can be judged by studying the 2nd from the 9th, his ills the 6th from the 9th, his profession the 10th from the 9th and so on."

Fosterage and adoption:

  • Saturn owning the 9th, in a movable sign, unaspected by benefics → the child lives under the care of a foster-father;
  • the 9th or its lord in a movable sign, occupied or aspected by Saturn, with a strong lord of the 11th → the child is sure to be adopted by another.

Brothers and sisters: the 3rd rules brothers with Mars as karaka; the 11th rules sisters. "The presence of Mars in the 3rd does not indicate many brothers." The number of brothers "may be ascertained from the number of Navamsas gained by the lord of the 3rd or by Mars whichever is stronger" — that is, the count of navamsas traversed in its sign.

Enemies and debts: the 6th governs both.

Rule Indication
malefics in the 6th success over enemies and redemption from debts
6th empty of all planets absence of enemies
Ascendant lord conjunct the 6th, aspected by the 6th lord much suffering from cousins and enemies
4th lord conjunct the 8th lord the native is likely to be charged with criminal offences
4th lord in good relation with the 11th lord harmonious relations with friends

"Debts will be incurred either during the periods (Dasas) or sub-periods (Bhuktis) of planets ruling the 6th, the 8th and the 12th or of those planets who may be in conjunction with or aspected by the lords of the 6th, the 8th and/or the 12th."

10.8 Marriage (chapter IX)

"In judging the affairs of marriage, consideration should be bestowed upon Venus as he is lord of the wife or husband (Kalatrakaraka) and the 7th house and the lord of the 7th."

A planet in the 7th:

Planet Partner
Sun proud, firm-minded; delays in marriage
Moon kind and domesticated, wavering mind
Mars quarrelsome, domineering; frequent separation by quarrels
Mercury quick-minded, bad-tempered if afflicted
Jupiter good, and in general a happy marriage
Venus happy if Venus unafflicted; otherwise quarrels and strong attraction to the opposite sex
Saturn difference of age or station; generally an unhappy married life
Rahu, Ketu tragic conditions

Other rules:

  • "The lord of the 7th should not occupy the 6th, or the 8th and the 2nd should not be aspected by malefics, to assure marital happiness."
  • 7th hemmed between two malefics (Papakarthari) → married life "burdensome and miserable".
  • Venus afflicted by Mars → "tumultuous feelings and little emotional calm or happiness with prolonged separation".
  • bad aspects between the 5th and 7th lords → lifelong celibacy.
  • Sun and Rahu in the 7th → loss of wealth through association with women.
  • malefics in the 4th, 7th and 12th → bereft of wife and children.
  • Ascendant lord in the 7th under benefic influence → a partner from a respectable family.
  • Moon in the Ascendant or the 7th with Leo rising in Navamsa → the partner's character "becomes questionable".
  • Venus well disposed in the 7th → good fortune after marriage.
  • malefics in the 8th, particularly Mars → early widowhood.
  • Saturn in the 8th → an unromantic, charmless married life.
  • Moon with Saturn in the 7th → the woman will have more than one marriage.
  • 7th a common sign with Venus conjunct malefics → more than one marriage invariably.
  • Ascendant lord and 7th lord close together → early marriage; likewise benefics in the 1st, 2nd and 7th.

Timing: marriage occurs during the dasha of (1) a planet in the 7th; (2) Venus or the Moon; (3) a planet aspecting the 7th; or (4) the lord of the 7th.

10.9 Children (chapter IX)

"The 5th house rules children. Jupiter indicates progeny and the 5th lord is also connected with issues. Favourable disposition of all these three factors is a great asset for getting good, long-lived and obedient children."

Rule Indication
5th (from Lagna or Moon) occupied by well-aspected benefics children a source of happiness, long-lived
5th occupied by malefics or heavily afflicted the opposite
5th lord exalted, well aspected, in a congenial sign illustrious children
malefics in the 12th of a female chart dangers in confinement
Jupiter, Ascendant lord and the 7th and 5th lords all weak and afflicted no children should be predicted
Saturn in the 5th with the 5th lord conjunct Rahu no surviving children
5th house and lord in a male sign, or conjunct/aspected by male planets mostly sons
5th house and lord in a female sign, or conjunct/aspected by female planets daughters
Jupiter or the 5th lord in friendly, inimical or depression navamsas the number of issues

The navamsa count: "The number of Navamsas, gained by Jupiter or lord of the 5th indicates the number of issues to be born." The book's worked example: "Mercury, the lord of the 5th, has gained seven Navamsas. Birth of seven children is denoted, out of which surviving will be five or six as two malefics intervene in between Aries and Libra in the Navamsa diagram."

Timing: births occur in the dasha or bhukti of Jupiter, or of the 5th lord (from Lagna or Moon), or of a planet aspecting the 5th.

CAUTION: the navamsa-count method for the number of children is the least reliable rule in the book and the one most likely to be read as a prediction about a real family. The engine does not implement it.
