Knowledge base

Fifteen documents extracted from the book and reorganised by subject, because the book keeps circling back — friendship appears in chapters I and III, the Navamsa in II and IX, Saturn in IV, X and XII.

Statements are tagged with where they come from: SOURCE is stated in the book, STANDARD is common practice the book assumes but does not spell out, and CAUTION marks a place where a 1930s primer should not be anyone's last word. Where the scan is damaged, the gap is shown rather than filled from another book.

How the engine uses this

Every rule the reading applies has an id and a chapter reference, so a statement in a chart can be traced back to a page here. The rule ids are listed in src/jyotish/interpret.py, and the data tables the engine runs on — exaltations, moolatrikonas, friendships, house significations, the gochara tables — are in src/jyotish/zodiac.py and src/jyotish/transits.py. The browser build shares them through site/assets/js/data.js, which is generated from the Python source so the two cannot drift apart.
