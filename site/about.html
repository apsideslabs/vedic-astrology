Provenance and limits

What this is built from, what the scan damaged, and what it therefore cannot do.

01The source

B. V. Raman, Astrology for Beginners — 135 pages, twelve chapters, two appendices, published by UBS Publishers' Distributors, New Delhi. The copy this was extracted from is an Internet Archive scan recoded by LuraDocument in 2017, carrying an OCR text layer of about 147,000 characters. Pages 1, 6, 8 and 10 are near-empty; pages 60–63 are duplicated in the scan itself.

The text was extracted page by page, the running watermark stripped, the one-word-per-line OCR layout reflowed, and the content reorganised by subject rather than by chapter — the book returns to the same topics repeatedly. Every table was transcribed by hand.

03What is missing on purpose

Ashtakavarga, full Shadbala, divisional charts beyond D9, compatibility matching and muhurta are all absent — they are not in this book.

Two rules are documented but deliberately not implemented: the navamsa count for the number of brothers and children, and the Sade Sati first / second / third passage reading — “during the third time the death of the native is invariably caused”. Both are the rules most likely to be read as a prediction about a real family or a real death.

04How it is checked

The book works a chart out by hand in chapter II and the engine reproduces it: nine grahas within a few arcminutes, the Rasi chart, the dignities, the Mars–Jupiter exchange, and the Vimshottari balance of 6 years 1 month 15 days. Thirty-four tests assert this.

The browser build is checked the same way: tools/verify_js.js and tools/verify_py.py dump the same chart from both implementations and compare them field by field. They agree exactly — positions, houses, dignities, dasha balance, panchanga, yogas, aspects, transits and the structure of the reading.

05Copyright

The book is under copyright and is not reproduced here. What is here is a structured summary of its rules — the extraction a student would make for their own study — together with code written from scratch. The code is MIT licensed. The prose in the knowledge base is a derivative summary, provided for study and commentary.

06What it is for

Study and self-reflection. Not medical, financial or legal advice, and not a factual prediction about any person. The book says as much itself:

“The judgment of a horoscope forms the most important and difficult task of an astrologer. It must be borne in mind that, at the outset no hard and fast rules can be laid down for the guidance of the reader.”
