Cast a chart

The chart is computed in your browser — no request leaves the page. Defaults are set to the book's own worked example; change them to your own birth data.

Defaults reproduce the chart in chapter II of the book: 2 November 1935, 05:20 local mean time at Madras, latitude 13° N, longitude 5h 10m 20s east (77.5833°), which is a UT offset of 5.1722 hours. The book uses its own ayanamsa rule, so that model is selected.
